## 2. Data processed
- Identification and contact data: first name, last name, email address, phone number;
- age (and, for children under 14, the name of the parent or guardian giving consent);
- booking request data (chosen package, requested dates and times, notes, indicative price);
- invoicing data: name or company name of the invoice holder, tax code and/or VAT number, address, SDI recipient code or PEC. The holder may be someone other than the student (e.g. a parent): in that case whoever enters the data confirms they have informed that person;
- technical data strictly necessary for the site to work (technical session cookie).

## 3. Purposes and legal basis
| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Handling the booking request, contacting you to confirm the dates and complete the order | Pre-contractual measures requested by the data subject (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) |
| Verifying the email address with a code | Pre-contractual measures (Art. 6.1.b) |
| Issuing the invoice and other tax and administrative obligations in case of purchase | Legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c) |

Data is **not** used for marketing purposes nor transferred to third parties.

## 4. Methods and retention
Data is processed electronically with appropriate security measures. It is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and any resulting relationship and, afterwards, for the period required by law (e.g. 10 years for tax documents). Requests not followed by a purchase are deleted within 24 months.

## 5. Recipients
Data may be processed by technical service providers (hosting, email delivery) appointed as processors. Invoicing data is communicated, as required by law, to the Italian Revenue Agency through the e-invoicing exchange system (SdI) and possibly to the controller's tax advisor. Data is stored within the European Union.

## 6. Provision of data
Providing the data, including invoicing data, is necessary to send the booking request: without it the request cannot be handled and no invoice can be issued.

## 7. Children
For participants under 14, consent is given by the parent or the holder of parental responsibility.

## 8. Your rights
You may at any time request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and object to processing (Articles 15-22 GDPR) by writing to the contact in section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

## 9. Cookies
The site only uses a technical session cookie, needed to keep you signed in after email verification. No profiling or third-party cookies are used.
